About Animals


Animals are multicellular organisms that belong to the kingdom Animalia. They are characterized by their ability to move, consume organic material for energy, and reproduce sexually.


Animals play a crucial role in ecosystems by acting as predators, prey, pollinators, and contributors to nutrient cycles. They exhibit a wide range of behaviors and adaptations that allow them to survive in diverse environments.


Animals can be found in almost every environment, from the depths of the oceans to the highest mountains. Protecting wildlife and their habitats is important for maintaining healthy ecosystems.
